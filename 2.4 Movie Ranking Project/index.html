2. Silent Witch: Chinmoku no Majo no Kakushigoto

Silent Witch: Chinmoku no Majo no Kakushigoto is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Yuki Okada. It has been serialized in Shogakukan's Weekly Shōnen Sunday since September 2020, with its chapters collected into 10 tankōbon volumes as of September 2023. The story follows a witch who has lost her voice and her journey to regain it.

3. solo leveling

Solo Leveling is a South Korean web novel written by Chugong. It was first published in 2016 and has since been adapted into a webtoon and a manhwa. The story follows Sung Jin-Woo, a weak hunter who gains the ability to level up and become stronger after surviving a dangerous dungeon.
